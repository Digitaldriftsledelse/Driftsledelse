# Summarize Notebook

Analyze the Jupyter notebook(s) in the current project and provide a structured summary including:

1. **Purpose** – What problem does the notebook solve?
2. **Key sections** – List the main sections/cells and what they do
3. **Data sources** – What data is loaded or used?
4. **Outputs** – What visualizations, tables, or results are produced?
5. **Dependencies** – What libraries and packages are imported?

Be concise. Use bullet points and headers. Focus on what matters for someone trying to understand the notebook quickly.
